# 🔬 Research Lab 01

# Reading Your First Machine Learning Research Paper

*"Theory becomes understanding when you practice it."*

In this lab, we'll apply the concepts learned in **Day 6 – Reading Research Papers** to a real machine learning research paper.

Instead of simply reading the paper, we'll learn how researchers analyze it using the Three-Pass Reading Strategy, evaluate its contributions, identify its strengths and limitations, and extract ideas for future research.

By the end of this lab, you'll understand not only **what** the paper says, but also **how** to think like a machine learning researcher while reading it.
