## 💡 Why This Paper?

This paper was selected because it directly complements **Day 6 – Reading Research Papers** in this roadmap.

It introduces the **Three-Pass Reading Strategy**, a simple and practical framework that helps researchers approach scientific papers without feeling overwhelmed. Rather than trying to understand every detail during the first read, the strategy encourages building understanding gradually through multiple passes.

Unlike highly technical machine learning papers, this paper focuses on developing an essential research skill that every student, engineer, and researcher can apply regardless of their field.

By mastering this reading strategy first, you'll be better prepared to understand more advanced machine learning research papers in the upcoming labs.

# 📚 What I Learned

After completing this lab, I learned that reading research papers is not about understanding every detail immediately.

Instead, it is about gradually building understanding by:

- understanding the research problem
- identifying the main contribution
- evaluating the methodology
- questioning the results
- thinking critically about future improvements

The biggest mindset shift for me was realizing that **research papers should be explored in layers rather than consumed in a single reading.**
